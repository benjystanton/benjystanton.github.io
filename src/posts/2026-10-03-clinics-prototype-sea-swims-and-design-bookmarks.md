## Clinics prototype

This week I’ve been focusing on creating a first prototype to explore how we might help clinics run more efficiently.

RAVS is efficient already, but we have some findings and feedback from users about it being frustrating sometimes when recording repeat vaccine events back-to-back, if they know in advance some things won’t change from patient to patient.

It’s really become 2 distinct prototype flows now.

One is an enhancement to our existing appointments feature that explores how front desk staff might “check-in” patients and begin the recording journey in advance.

The other explores how we might set default answers for clinics, e.g. when users visit a care home to do a mass vaccinations.

In both cases, our users might know who the patients are and what vaccination they are having in advance.

Our team found various design history posts that were really helpful so I’m going to save the rest of my write up and commit to starting a design history post next week.
