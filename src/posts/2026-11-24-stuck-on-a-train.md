## GovCamp Cymru 2026 anti-racism reading list

Since going to [GovCamp Cymru 2026](/blog/govcamp-cymru-2026/), I've been getting through a couple of books…

### Nice Racism

I've been listening to an audiobook of [Nice Racism](https://www.penguin.co.uk/books/444290/nice-racism-by-diangelo-robin/9780141997421). This has been an uncomfortable but massively valuable experience.

I've realised that my default state of being quiet, polite and nice in the face of racism is not something to be proud of, and I've got a lot of work to do if I ever want to consider myself an anti-racist. The book is full of practical advice that is helping me to reframe my thinking.

### Welsh (Plural)

I've also been reading [Welsh (Plural): Essays on the Future of Wales](https://repeaterbooks.com/product/welsh-plural-essays-on-the-future-of-wales/). Which also lead me to watch [Cyrraedd Cymru (arriving in Wales)](https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/p0ph9kq6) on BBC iPlayer.

Both explore, in part, the history of racism in Wales, and I'm ashamed to admit that I know almost nothing about the history of people who have immigrated to Wales.
