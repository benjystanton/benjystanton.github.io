## Dependabot checks

I was reviewing lots of Dependabot PRs (pull requests) this week. (Dependabot is an automated dependency and security tool built into GitHub). Keeping on top of them can feel like a bit of a chore, but I've decided to be more proactive with them.

I decided to write out a list of minimum steps to follow every time. I came up with the following (but interested if anyone does anything else?):

- check branch is up to date with main
- check change is limited to technical dependancies (e.g. the `package-lock.json` file)
- check GitHub's automatic checks have passed
- check the preview deployment link is successful
- manually check a selection of pages (compare them to the live site / main branch deployment)

## Cardiff Half and Alton Towers

2 weeks ago I ran Cardiff Half for the second time. I found it much harder than last time. I don't think I paced myself very well. I was only training once a week which is not enough.

Last weekend we were at Alton Towers for my son's 10th birthday. I was a bit dubious about going but really enjoyed it! The whole family are old enough to go on rollercoasters together now, and there are loads at Alton Towers so it was great for the 5 of us to be all together for 2 whole days.

I think that, perhaps counter-intuitively, being stuck in a 80 minute queue with nothing to do except chat is part of the draw with theme parks, at least for parents. Hard to get that much undivided attention from teenagers any other way.
